  • Periodic events: craft fairs, cultural gatherings

Location advantages

  • Quiririm has Italian heritage — strong local identity
  • En route to Campos do Jordão — natural weekend tourist flow
  • Part of rural tourism circuits
  • Car-accessible from central Taubaté
  • Under-served neighbourhood for the 30–50 female demographic

Strategic goals for Instagram

Three objectives, in order of priority

  • Generate planned visits — the audience chooses Fabbrica in advance, schedules with a friend, and makes it a recurring outing
  • Justify a higher ticket — communicate the full experience (space + food + coffee + conversation), not just price-driven impulse
  • Build Wednesday–Thursday demand — create content specifically targeting weekday afternoon visits to reduce peak/off-peak imbalance

The gap: None of the three local competitors speaks to women who want an authentic, delicious, and comfortable café for a weekend outing or a weekday afternoon with friends. That territory is completely open in Quiririm.

Strategic implication: Fabbrica should not try to out-do Empório Q! on sophistication or Santa Justa on femininity. The winning position is authentic warmth — the place that feels real, beautiful, and genuinely good. Not pretentious. The kind of place you want to tag and tell a friend about.

Primary persona

Women aged 30–50, Vale do Paraíba

  • Goes out on weekday afternoons or early evenings with friends
  • Visits cafés and restaurants on weekends with a partner or group
  • Loves good food, sweets, and anything visually beautiful
  • Has aesthetic taste but is not affected — not a "clean girl" or minimalist
  • Lives in Vale do Paraíba — appreciates homemade and characterful things
  • Uses Instagram to discover, plan, and share outings with people she cares about

What she does on Instagram

Discovery behaviour

  • Scrolls Reels for inspiration and "places to go"
  • Saves content she wants to act on later
  • Sends Reels to friends as invitations ("wanna go?")
  • Looks for visual proof that a place is worth her time

Planning behaviour

  • Researches a place before going — checks photos, vibe, menu
  • Plans outings in advance, especially mid-week
  • Chooses places she can photograph and share
  • Wants a full experience, not just a coffee

Visual identity guidelines

✓ Do

  • Warm, amber-toned colour grade
  • Natural light — morning or golden hour
  • Textured backgrounds: clay, wood, linen
  • Tight close-ups of food and hands
  • Consistent colour temperature across all posts
  • 4:5 or 9:16 aspect ratio for all posts

✗ Don't

  • Cold, blue-tinted or flat light
  • Minimalist/clean aesthetic — not on brand
  • Watermarks from other apps (TikTok, CapCut)
  • Cluttered captions with 20+ hashtags
  • Inconsistent colour grades across posts
  • Stock-looking or over-edited imagery

Caption strategy

How to write captions that convert

  • First line is everything — the hook must be visible before the "more" button. Make it emotional or curiosity-driven, not descriptive.
  • Write for sharing — "tag someone who needs this" or "send this to your coffee friend" consistently outperform informational captions.
  • 3–5 hashtags max — 2026 data confirms hashtag stuffing is penalised. Use hyper-specific ones: #cafequiririm, #cafetaubate, #valedoparaiba.
  • Keywords in captions matter — Instagram indexes captions for search. Include "café Quiririm", "café Taubaté", "café artesanal".
  • Call to action every post — save, share, comment, or visit. One CTA per post, never all four.

2026 shift: Instagram now runs multiple separate AI ranking systems — one each for Feed, Reels, Stories, and Explore. Understanding which signals matter for each is the difference between organic growth and invisible content.

The 3 ranking signals (confirmed by Adam Mosseri, Jan 2025)

#1 Signal

Watch time

How long people watch, especially past the first 3 seconds. A 10s Reel with 80% retention beats a 60s Reel with 30% retention every time.

#2 Followers

Likes per reach

For connected reach (your existing followers), the % of viewers who like your post matters. Focus on content your audience actually enjoys.

#1 New audience

Sends per reach

DM shares are the strongest signal for reaching non-followers. 694,000 Reels are shared via DM every minute. Make content worth forwarding.

The critical first 3 seconds

Hook strategy — your most important creative decision

Up to 50% of viewers drop off in the first 3 seconds. Instagram reads this as a signal not to distribute the content further. Aim for 60%+ retention past the 3-second mark.

2026 algorithm updates you need to know

"Your Algorithm" — Dec 2025

Users now explicitly choose topics they want to see. Reels that match selected topics see 40–50% higher reach. This means Fabbrica's content must be clearly categorised — food, coffee, local, lifestyle — not generic.

Views as primary metric

Instagram officially transitioned to Views as the main metric across all formats. Track views, not just likes. A post with 500 views and 50 likes outperforms one with 200 views and 40 likes.

Trial Reels

Test a Reel with non-followers only before publishing to your audience. If it performs well in 24 hours, push it broadly. Use this to test new hook styles or experimental content without risking your engagement rate.

Google indexing

Since mid-2025, Instagram posts are indexed by Google. Use keywords in captions ("café artesanal Taubaté") and alt text to capture search traffic beyond Instagram itself.

Reels length

7–30 seconds = best for new audience reach (high completion rate). 30–90 seconds = better for deeper storytelling with existing followers. Never exceed 90 seconds unless retention data is exceptional.

No watermarks

Content with TikTok or CapCut watermarks is actively penalised and will not be recommended to new audiences. Edit natively in Instagram or use watermark-free tools.

Promotion types

Organic — always on

DM-share bait content

  • Design every food and experience Reel to be forwarded
  • End with "send this to someone you'd bring here"
  • Track sends per Reel in Insights — this is your best growth signal
  • Reply to every comment in the first hour of posting
Paid — Meta Ads

Boosting top-performing Reels

  • Only boost Reels that already have 60%+ 3-second retention
  • Target: women 28–52, within 40km of Taubaté
  • Interest targeting: specialty coffee, weekend outings, food
  • Budget: R$15–25/day, Thursday–Sunday only
  • Objective: Video views or Reach (not Likes)
Organic — local SEO

Geolocation & keyword strategy

  • Always tag location: Quiririm, Taubaté
  • Use keywords in captions: "café Quiririm", "café artesanal Taubaté"
  • Posts are now indexed by Google — optimise for search
  • Engage with local hashtags: #taubate #quiririm #valedoparaiba
Earned — micro influencers

Local creator partnerships

  • Target creators with 3K–30K followers in Vale do Paraíba
  • Invite them for a free experience in exchange for content
  • Focus on food/lifestyle/travel creators, not just "influencers"
  • Ask them to tag location and use relevant hashtags
  • Repost their content in Stories immediately

Wednesday–Thursday demand strategy

Post a Reel on Tuesday at midday specifically framed around weekday afternoons: "The best time to come is when it's quiet." Show the space with no queue, the best light, the full attention of the team. Follow with a Story on Wednesday morning: "We're here. Come in." This creates a content loop that directly targets the low-footfall days.

Summary

A Fabbrica Café has a rare combination: a completely unique physical space and a fully open market position — women aged 30–50 who want a comfortable, authentic, and delicious café to visit with friends or a partner. Instagram should be built as the primary acquisition channel, generating desire, enabling visit planning, and encouraging organic sharing. The bet is on the space's authenticity and the quality of its products — exactly what this audience responds to.
